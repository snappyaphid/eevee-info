Eevee

Eevee is a private personal assistant. It is not a public service: it runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. No one else can sign up or sign in.

It reads its owner's email (read-only) so it can send short summaries of important messages to the owner's phone.

It reads its owner's YouTube channel statistics (read-only) to show them on a private dashboard.
